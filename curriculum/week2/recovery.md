# Week 2 — Recovery with evidence

Stop at the first failed check. Keep the command, exit status, and smallest
useful output. Do not claim success because a program is installed or a model
appears in a list. Bring the receipt to [Recitation / Get Help](../recitation.md).

The canonical paths remain `local_ai_lab_setup` for the instructional product
and `windows_classroom` for Windows evidence. Do not invent a third procedure
or silently change the model tag. If the environment is not ready, say
`NOT READY`, preserve the evidence, and ask for help.
