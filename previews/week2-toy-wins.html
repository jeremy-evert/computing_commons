LOCAL AI LAB · STEP 10

Practice three tiny Aider wins

Repeat the same safe loop until the evidence habit feels ordinary.

Do this first

  1. Visible edit: make one requested, observable change.
  2. Test-backed repair: repair the supplied failing behavior.
  3. Harmless modification: ask for one student-directed, low-risk change.

For every win: inspect → ask → diff → test.

Evidence of success

For each win you can say what changed, what the diff showed, what the test proved, and what remains unproven.

If this fails

Stop after the first unexpected result. Keep the smallest useful receipt and ask at Recitation.
