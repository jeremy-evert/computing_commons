FRIDAY · PROFESSIONAL MINDS

Mindset at work

Read the canonical session on challenge, feedback, leadership, and learning at work. Mindset is about how people respond to evidence—not a motivational slogan.

Do this

  1. Open the canonical Friday reading in this module and read it before discussion.
  2. Identify one response to failure that protects appearances and one that supports learning.
  3. Apply the distinction to a debugging, review, or help-seeking moment from the Local AI runway.
  4. Describe one concrete next action that uses feedback without silencing disagreement.

What this means for computing

A failed command, bad output, or review comment can become information for the next experiment. A healthy team can confront hard truths, invite criticism, and develop people; it does not pretend every setback proves a person’s fixed ability. Keep the nuance from the source and distinguish a learning-oriented response from lowering standards.

Keep the receipt

Save one specific example of a mistake or feedback moment, the response you observed or would choose, and the next bounded recovery step. Connect it to Monday’s context habit and Wednesday’s retrieval habit.

Need help or recovery?

Bring the failed attempt, not only the polished result, to Recitation / Get Help. Asking for help is part of the professional practice.
