Friday · Professional Minds — Mindset at Work READY

Book/source: Mindset / mindset-at-work source set. reading · projector deck

Student route: Friday — Mindset at Work

  • Watch for reducing mindset to “try harder.”
  • Evidence: a concrete feedback moment and bounded recovery action.
