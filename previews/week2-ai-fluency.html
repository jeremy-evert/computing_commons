MONDAY · AI FLUENCY LEVEL 1

Gather Context

AI output changes when you supply the facts that change the task. More words are not automatically better context.

Do this

  1. Run the same fictional task with no context, some context, and rich context.
  2. For each version, write down what the AI still had to guess.
  3. Identify the one piece of context that most changed the answer.
  4. Leave out one true detail that would only add padding.

What this means

Useful context can include audience, purpose, background, constraints, examples, data, tone, and format. A relevant fact changes what a good answer should be; a merely related fact makes the request longer. Claims and generated output still require human verification.

This is the canonical context-comparison move in a small Commons wrapper, not a replacement assignment: keep the three requests, the comparison, and the deliberate omission.

Keep the receipt

Save the three requests, representative responses or fallback comparison, what each version forced the AI to guess, and your “most important context” answer.

Need recovery?

Use a fictional scenario; do not paste private data. If the tool is unavailable, use the prepared non-AI fallback. Bring your comparison sheet and uncertainty to Recitation.
