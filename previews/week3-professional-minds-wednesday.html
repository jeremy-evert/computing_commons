WEDNESDAY · PROFESSIONAL MINDS · GROWTH

Grow through mistakes

Read, discuss, and apply the *Limitless Mind* perspective on productive struggle without turning it into a slogan.

Do this

  1. Read the canonical Wednesday reading before class; view the Wednesday deck during discussion.
  2. Explain from memory why mistakes can support learning, then check your explanation against the source.
  3. Choose a task at the edge of your understanding. Name one useful mistake, the correction you would examine, and the next attempt.
  4. Reflect on how this applies to debugging, failed checks, recovery, and asking for help.

Keep the nuance

The reading does not say every failure is beneficial, that struggle should continue without support, or that disappointment is unreal. It says challenge becomes useful when people notice errors, revise, use resources, and try again in an environment that preserves meaningful difficulty.

Keep the receipt

Save your retrieval attempt, the gap or mistake it exposed, the correction, and one specific next practice. Keep the reading response if your instructor requests it.

If the mistake feels personal

Bring the exact failed check, unclear concept, or feedback—not a polished story—to Recitation / Get Help. Evidence can guide recovery without becoming an identity judgment.
