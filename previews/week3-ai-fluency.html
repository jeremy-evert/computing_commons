MONDAY · AI FLUENCY LEVEL 1

Plan the Work

Before asking for a draft, make the route visible enough to inspect and improve.

Do this

  1. Carry forward one task, useful fact, and consequential gap or assumption from Week 2.
  2. Write an objective, ordered stages, needed information, decision points or dependencies, risks, and quality checkpoints.
  3. For a decision, make a table and trace boundary values before drafting. For the shared example, check -1, 0, 1, 10, and 11.
  4. Critique the route, record one visible revision, then write the plain-language rule or next-draft statement.

What this means

A plan is a hypothesis about the work, not proof that the work will succeed. It makes missing facts, decisions, risks, and checks visible while they are still cheap to change. Planning the whole route is different from listing functions, owners, or interfaces; that is a later decomposition question.

Use the canonical Monday activity and portfolio artifact in this module. AI may help critique a plan, but it does not replace your judgment or write your CS1 work.

Keep the receipt

Save the completed Plan-first Workflow, including the original route, critique, revision, quality check, and final plain-language write. Keep it with your earlier portfolio artifacts.

Need recovery?

Use the prepared study-room scenario if you lack a Week 2 handoff. If a tool is unavailable, complete the printed fallback. Do not paste private data. Bring the route and the smallest unclear step to Recitation / Get Help.
