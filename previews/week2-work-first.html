Bridge to Work First

The tool helps with bounded work; you keep direction, inspection, and durable truth.

Do this first

  1. Know the starting state.
  2. State one bounded desired change.
  3. Give only needed context.
  4. Let the tool do bounded work.
  5. Inspect diff and evidence.
  6. Test independently.
  7. Accept, repair, or recover.
  8. Preserve truth outside transient chat.

What this prepares you for

This is the small version of the later Work First station in the Recitation Toolbox. Do not attempt the entire station here.

Recovery is part of the loop. Keep the evidence and use Week 2 Recovery.
