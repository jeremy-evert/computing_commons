# Raw owner direction — Clearinghouse + progressive release

Date: 2026-08-23
Status: **OWNER DIRECTION**

The Computing Commons should function as a **clearinghouse**, not simply as another place to copy shared material.

Key owner direction:

- `computer_science_1` should increasingly contain only Computer Science I material. Shared/common content should move toward canonical shared homes and the Commons delivery layer.
- The Commons must stitch together the existing Local AI work rather than duplicate it, especially:
  - `jeremy-evert/local_ai_lab_setup`
  - `jeremy-evert/windows_classroom`
- The Commons must stitch in `jeremy-evert/ai_fluency` rather than using course-local Monday Moment copies as canonical curriculum.
- Professional Minds is also a shared strand and should use its shared source rather than six course-local copies.
- The level model is **progressive/cumulative**. If students are asked to take Level 4, Levels 1, 2, 3, and 4 should be available. Earlier levels remain open for review/recovery; later levels need not be exposed until assigned.
- Fall 2026 can remain a soft launch with due/trackable 0-point Commons assignments and bonus credit awarded through home courses for strong work/evidence.
- Student enrollment into/out of the Commons Canvas course may be managed by course/semester, but that operational behavior should be tested before becoming policy.

The architectural goal is one coherent student-facing Recitation surface that consumes the strongest shared repositories while allowing course repositories to become more focused on their actual disciplines.
