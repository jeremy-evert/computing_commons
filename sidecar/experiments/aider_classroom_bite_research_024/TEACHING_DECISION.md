Do not add an edit-mode rule to the freshman card yet. The instructor cookbook may note provisionally that Aider's `diff` transport is worth a clean replication on a Module-sized bite because it produced correct target edits faster in the two complete scored observations, but it did not pass the placeholder-path gate and the third repetition did not complete.

Keep the supported rule from Mission 023: small job + small workspace. Transport is a promising instructor/configuration variable, not a student-facing recipe. The next and only variable should be a repaired, clean replication of the same whole-versus-diff Module comparison.
