# Week 3 shared-rhythm teaching guide

Use the Commons pages to orient the week. The canonical AI Fluency and Professional Minds assets remain the teaching authorities; the wrappers give students one route, one small receipt per day, and a recovery seam.

## Monday — AI Fluency Level 1: Plan the Work

- **Purpose:** Make the whole route visible before drafting: objective, stages, needed information, decisions/dependencies, risks, and checks.
- **Say:** “A plan is a hypothesis about the work, not proof that the work will succeed.”
- **Ask:** “Which boundary or missing fact would expose a bad route fastest?”
- **Student action:** Carry forward Week 2 context, complete the Plan-first Workflow, critique it, revise it, and write the plain-language result.
- **Evidence:** Workflow with a concrete checkpoint, visible revision, and final statement; use `-1/0/1/10/11` for the shared branching example.
- **Watch for:** A component/interface inventory instead of a whole-task route; drafting before checking; “test it” without a concrete case.
- **Move on when:** Each student can point to a changed route and explain why it is safer or clearer.

Source assets: `ai_fluency/ai_i/monday_moments/week_03_plan_the_work/` (all five Markdown files), source commit `022262cf207c28a9504425779a24247ddcf66884`.

## Wednesday — Professional Minds: Growth through mistakes

- **Purpose:** Connect productive struggle to debugging, failed checks, revision, and help-seeking without reducing the source to “try harder.”
- **Say:** “A mistake is evidence only when we inspect it, correct something, and decide what to try next.”
- **Ask:** “What did the error reveal about the method, and what support preserves the challenge?”
- **Student action:** Read the canonical article, retrieve one idea without notes, discuss the deck, and apply the idea to a real-but-safe technical learning situation.
- **Evidence:** Retrieval gap, useful mistake, correction, and next bounded attempt.
- **Watch for:** Treating failure as proof of identity; pretending every failure is good; removing all difficulty instead of providing support.
- **Move on when:** Students can name both the needed revision and the support or resource that makes a next attempt possible.

Source assets: `professional_minds/readings/week_03_wed_growing_through_mistakes.md` and `presentations/beamer/week_03_wed/week03_wed.pdf`, source commit `af54438aeb4bddbbceed4524bc10379b0b9d5a3c`.

## Friday — Professional Minds: Resilience and risk competence

- **Purpose:** Treat resilience as relational and systemic, and connect accurate information to bounded recovery and responsible judgment.
- **Say:** “Resilience is not a private toughness score; risk competence joins information with the ability to respond in context.”
- **Ask:** “What should we do differently, and what assumption or team pattern shaped our response?”
- **Student action:** Read/view the source, identify the three associated factors, apply risk competence to a technical challenge, and complete a double-loop reflection.
- **Evidence:** Challenge, observations, bounded action/help request, and the assumption/norm/relationship surfaced.
- **Watch for:** Pop-psychology shorthand, individual blame, risk warnings without situational judgment, or pressure to disclose unsafe/private details.
- **Move on when:** Students can distinguish a recovery action from a vague promise to persist and can identify a team/system condition.

Source assets: `professional_minds/readings/week_03_fri_resilience_and_risk_competence.md` and `presentations/beamer/week_03_fri/week03_fri.pdf`, source commit `af54438aeb4bddbbceed4524bc10379b0b9d5a3c`. The reading is an explicitly labeled companion-source substitution, not the unavailable complete *Resilience Education* book.

## Show this on Monday

Open the Week 3 shared-rhythm page and preview the Wednesday card: students will read/view the *Limitless Mind* companion, retrieve one idea, then connect a mistake to a correction and next attempt. Say that Wednesday is about learning from evidence with support, not celebrating failure.

## Recovery/help route

Send students to Recitation / Get Help with goal, attempt, observation, conclusion, and next question. A student without a device, AI account, source file, or safe example can use the printed canonical fallback and the same receipt.

## What not to improvise live

Do not rewrite canonical readings, ask AI to produce student code, promise generated output is correct, turn resilience into “be tougher,” or require private wellbeing disclosures. Keep source limitations visible and use evidence to decide whether the next step worked.
