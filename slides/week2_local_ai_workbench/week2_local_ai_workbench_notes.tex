\slide{1. Build and Verify Your Local AI Workbench}\field{Purpose}{Set the finish line: evidence, not installation theater.}\field{Say}{Today we trace a local request, make one bounded change, and prove a result independently. Every check has a limit.}\field{Ask}{What would count as evidence that the workflow works?}\field{Do}{Keep a small receipt.}\field{Watch for}{A green status or generated answer becoming a conclusion.}\field{Move on when}{Students can name observation, claim, and limit.}
\slide{2. Today's finish line}\field{Purpose}{Preview trace, narrow change, and independent proof.}\field{Say}{We practice a repeatable loop rather than mastering every tool.}\field{Ask}{Which step is easiest to skip?}\field{Do}{Predict evidence for each outcome.}\field{Watch for}{Aider action being treated as correctness.}\field{Move on when}{Students can repeat the evidence ladder.}
\slide{3. Why these tools? The workbench is infrastructure}\field{Purpose}{Frame tools as shared infrastructure and judgment as human work.}\field{Say}{Machines provide motion; people provide meaning, scope, and acceptance.}\field{Ask}{Where does responsibility remain?}\field{Do}{Connect the rule to debugging or documentation.}\field{Watch for}{“The tool said so.”}\field{Move on when}{One human judgment is named.}
\slide{4. The stack: layers, not magic}\field{Purpose}{Establish request, client, service, model, and machine.}\field{Say}{A working layer does not prove the next layer. Check the chain in order.}\field{Ask}{What remains unproven?}\field{Do}{Name each arrow’s observable.}\field{Watch for}{Aider being called the model.}\field{Move on when}{Roles are distinct.}
\slide{5. Evidence before model action}\field{Purpose}{Separate foundation/service observations from inference.}\field{Say}{python --version proves a runtime answered; ollama list proves availability is reported; loopback proves reachability.}\field{Ask}{What remains unproven?}\field{Do}{Write one bounded claim per observation.}\field{Watch for}{“Installed” becoming “works.”}\field{Move on when}{All three limits are stated.}
\slide{6. Evidence after model action}\field{Purpose}{Separate inference and diff evidence.}\field{Say}{A request proves completion of one request; a diff proves text changed. Neither proves behavior.}\field{Ask}{What independent check supports behavior?}\field{Do}{Save tag, response, status, and diff.}\field{Watch for}{Trusting narration.}\field{Move on when}{Generation, change, and behavior are distinct.}
\slide{7. A chain of bounded evidence}\field{Purpose}{Consolidate observation, claim, check, and limit.}\field{Say}{We build a chain, not reassuring green lights.}\field{Ask}{What does this prove and not prove?}\field{Do}{Have one student supply a limit.}\field{Watch for}{Overclaiming from several observations.}\field{Move on when}{The chain can be narrated.}
\slide{8. First: open PowerShell}\field{Purpose}{Make the Windows action recognizable.}\field{Say}{Find PowerShell 7 and the prompt before typing.}\field{Ask}{How can you verify the shell?}\field{Do}{Point to Start, PowerShell 7, and prompt.}\field{Watch for}{Wrong shell or search box.}\field{Move on when}{Everyone identifies the prompt.}
\slide{9. Verify PowerShell itself}\field{Purpose}{Practice command, source, observation, and receipt.}\field{Say}{The version command answers one narrow question.}\field{Ask}{What official documentation supports it?}\field{Do}{Run the command and save smallest output.}\field{Watch for}{Screenshot substitution.}\field{Move on when}{Own output and limit are recorded.}
\slide{10. Python: the runtime for our program}\field{Purpose}{Make the runtime boundary explicit.}\field{Say}{Version output does not prove packages, program behavior, or tests.}\field{Ask}{What separate check tests behavior?}\field{Do}{Run the supplied foundation check.}\field{Watch for}{Version as validation.}\field{Move on when}{The untested claim is written.}
\slide{11. Git: the before-and-after witness}\field{Purpose}{Establish baseline and diff.}\field{Say}{Git is a witness, not a behavior judge.}\field{Ask}{Which file and lines changed?}\field{Do}{Run status and diff.}\field{Watch for}{Unrelated files or secrets.}\field{Move on when}{Baseline and intended file are recorded.}
\slide{12. Ollama: a local service on loopback}\field{Purpose}{Explain locality without overclaiming security.}\field{Say}{127.0.0.1 and an answer establish reachability, not inference, privacy, or integration.}\field{Ask}{What endpoint and response were observed?}\field{Do}{Run only the approved check.}\field{Watch for}{Port scans or cloud fallback.}\field{Move on when}{Reachability and limit are stated.}
\slide{13. Security is a judgment question}\field{Purpose}{Make safety and security an evidence conversation.}\field{Say}{Local is a routing fact; security includes configuration, authentication, data handling, and correctness.}\field{Ask}{Is this safe? Is this secure? How do I know? Where can I verify that?}\field{Do}{Compare official documentation with local observation.}\field{Watch for}{Loopback as a blanket privacy guarantee.}\field{Move on when}{Locality, configuration, and data handling are distinguished.}
\slide{14. The exact approved model: qwen3:8b}\field{Purpose}{Preserve the exact reviewed boundary.}\field{Say}{Listed means reported available; inference means one request answered.}\field{Ask}{What did inference add?}\field{Do}{Check exact tag and run approved request.}\field{Watch for}{Substitution or download.}\field{Move on when}{Tag, command, response, and status are saved.}
\slide{15. Aider is the coding client, not the model}\field{Purpose}{Clarify client/service/model roles.}\field{Say}{The approved path is \texttt{ollama\_chat/qwen3:8b} at \texttt{http://127.0.0.1:11434} with \texttt{num\_ctx: 8192}.}\field{Ask}{What does \texttt{aider --version} not prove?}\field{Do}{Confirm the local profile only.}\field{Watch for}{Cloud keys or provider prompts.}\field{Move on when}{Roles and boundary are named.}
\slide{16. The first Aider win: one bounded change}\field{Purpose}{Show scope as safety.}\field{Say}{A good request names one exercise, file, and behavior.}\field{Ask}{What is the smallest useful request?}\field{Do}{Record baseline and forbid test edits.}\field{Watch for}{Scope creep.}\field{Move on when}{File and behavior are clear.}
\slide{17. Inspect the diff before you trust it}\field{Purpose}{Place review between generation and acceptance.}\field{Say}{Aider changed it is not the same as correct.}\field{Ask}{Any extra file, secret, or cleanup?}\field{Do}{Exit and inspect the diff.}\field{Watch for}{Large unreviewed changes.}\field{Move on when}{Diff is bounded and explainable.}
\slide{18. Independent test: a different claim}\field{Purpose}{Separate behavior evidence from narration.}\field{Say}{A passing test supports only its defined behavior.}\field{Ask}{What does this test exercise?}\field{Do}{Run it and preserve status/output.}\field{Watch for}{Partial test as universal proof.}\field{Move on when}{Coverage boundary is stated.}
\slide{19. Three tiny wins: repeat the loop}\field{Purpose}{Build confidence through bounded cycles.}\field{Say}{Independence grows one baseline, ask, inspect, test, and recovery loop at a time.}\field{Ask}{Which evidence carries forward?}\field{Do}{Repeat the approved route.}\field{Watch for}{Increasing scope early.}\field{Move on when}{All five stages are narrated.}
\slide{20. Recovery is part of the workflow}\field{Purpose}{Normalize bounded stop and escalation.}\field{Say}{NOT READY is useful evidence. Stop, preserve, and retry once.}\field{Ask}{Which failures belong with staff?}\field{Do}{Practice a failure receipt.}\field{Watch for}{Switching models or repeated repair.}\field{Move on when}{A failure receipt exists.}
\slide{21. Prompt structure: from answer to evidence}\field{Purpose}{Turn vague questions into verifiable requests.}\field{Say}{Include platform, version, action, observation, source, and limits.}\field{Ask}{How would you request an independent check?}\field{Do}{Upgrade one weak question.}\field{Watch for}{Citation without local verification.}\field{Move on when}{The revised question has check and limit.}
\slide{22. Learning to fish: the scaffold fades}\field{Purpose}{Show increasing independence.}\field{Say}{The scaffold fades; claim, observation, limit, conclusion remain.}\field{Ask}{What responsibility increases?}\field{Do}{Place the task on the line.}\field{Watch for}{Less scaffold becoming less verification.}\field{Move on when}{One check is defended.}
\slide{23. Work First: the bridge}\field{Purpose}{Connect the exercise to professional practice.}\field{Say}{Work First preserves starting state, bounded work, evidence, tests, and durable truth.}\field{Ask}{Which step protects the next handoff?}\field{Do}{Map a receipt onto the pipeline.}\field{Watch for}{Documentation as optional.}\field{Move on when}{The loop connects to a handoff.}
\slide{24. Show that it works: the Week 2 receipt}\field{Purpose}{Define observable completion.}\field{Say}{A small receipt beats a confident story.}\field{Ask}{What is missing?}\field{Do}{Assemble and read receipts.}\field{Watch for}{Missing failure evidence or exact model.}\field{Move on when}{Change and test are demonstrated.}
\slide{25. Your turn}\field{Purpose}{Hand off to student work.}\field{Say}{Ask narrowly, inspect honestly, test independently, and keep the receipt.}\field{Ask}{What does this prove?}\field{Do}{Release the supplied exercise.}\field{Watch for}{Wrong folder or boundary changes.}\field{Move on when}{Baseline receipt has begun.}
